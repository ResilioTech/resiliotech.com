The Problem That Started It All

In 2018, Alex was CTO at a fast-growing fintech startup, and Sarah was leading engineering at a YC-backed SaaS company. Both of us were facing the same frustrating problem: spending more time fighting infrastructure fires than building the products our customers loved.

Alex's team was deploying manually every Friday night, praying nothing would break over the weekend. Sarah's company had raised $3M but couldn't hire senior DevOps talent fast enough to keep up with growth. Sound familiar?

We realized that thousands of startups were facing identical challenges—needing enterprise-grade DevOps practices but lacking the resources to build them in-house. Traditional consulting firms were too expensive and slow. Freelancers lacked the startup context. There had to be a better way.

"Every startup deserves to move fast without breaking things. DevOps shouldn't be a luxury reserved for unicorns with unlimited budgets." Alex Chen, Co-Founder & CTO
