Pricing FAQ

Why fixed pricing instead of hourly?

Fixed pricing gives you budget certainty and aligns our incentives with your success. We focus on delivering results, not billing hours. You know exactly what you'll pay upfront.

What if my project needs change during implementation?

Minor changes are included in our fixed price. For major scope changes, we'll discuss options and provide transparent pricing for any additions before proceeding.

Do you offer discounts for early-stage startups?

Yes! We offer extended payment terms, installment plans, and sometimes equity arrangements for qualified early-stage startups. Contact us to discuss your situation.

What happens if you can't deliver on time?

We guarantee delivery within the agreed timeline. If we're delayed due to our fault, we'll either accelerate delivery at no extra cost or provide partial refunds for delays.

Is ongoing maintenance included?

All packages include 30-90 days of support. After that, we offer maintenance packages starting at $500/month, or you can manage everything yourself with our documentation.

Can I upgrade my package later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade anytime and we'll credit your original investment toward the higher-tier package. Many clients start with Essential and upgrade as they grow.
